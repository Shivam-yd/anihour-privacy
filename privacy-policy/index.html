AniHour Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-11-16

About AniHour

AniHour is an anime and manga discovery app. It displays catalog information, cover images, recent episode schedules, and news. AniHour does not host or download anime episodes or manga chapters. Explicit adult titles are excluded; other catalog content can contain mature themes, violence, or suggestive imagery. AniHour is not designed specifically for children.

Information used to provide the app

No AniHour account is required. AniHour does not ask you to provide your name, email address, precise location, contacts, camera images, or microphone recordings. Search terms, selected filters, page numbers, and the titles you open are sent to AniList to retrieve the requested catalog information. Your device contacts third-party image hosts to load cover art and character images.

Network requests and service providers

The installed AniHour app retrieves catalog information, recent episode schedules, and airing/trending updates directly from AniList; it does not send these requests through an AniHour backend. AniList receives your requests and standard connection information, such as your IP address and request headers. Images are loaded directly from third-party image hosts, including YouTube for trailer thumbnails. These API, image, and network providers may process connection information and operational logs under their own privacy policies and retention practices. An internet connection is required.

Advertising and tracking

The current AniHour app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, an app-level analytics service, or an app-level crash-reporting service. It does not sell your information. The release configuration blocks access to the Android advertising ID. Development tools and Expo Go previews are separate from the standalone release and may have their own data practices.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Search results, selected filters, and the anime/manga selection are held in app memory during use. AniHour does not maintain a user account database or a saved search-history database. The operating system and image-loading libraries may cache images and other resources. You can clear cached app data through Android's app settings or uninstall AniHour. AniHour cannot delete records independently retained by AniList, external websites, or hosting providers; contact those services for their own retention and deletion policies.

Security and device permissions

Catalog requests and supported external links use HTTPS. AniHour does not need camera, microphone, photo-library, or location access for its discovery features, and the Android release configuration blocks those permissions. No method of network transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

External websites

Opening a trailer, news article, streaming-provider link, or other external link sends you to another service. That service may process information, set cookies, or require an account under its own privacy policy. AniHour does not control the content, privacy practices, availability, or charges of external websites.
